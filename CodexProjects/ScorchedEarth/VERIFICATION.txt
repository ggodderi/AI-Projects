Simulation coverage
- Reproducible procedural terrain, launch pads, bounded craters, airbursts.
- Gravity/wind integration and timestep equivalence.
- Collision against tanks, 1-pixel ridges, and world boundaries.
- Shell consumption, depleted-ammo fallback, and available-weapon cycling.
- Shield absorption, damage credits, friendly fire, and falling damage.
- Five-bomblet cluster split with no repeated splitting.
- Simultaneous destruction, round limits, draws, and match tiebreaks.
- Shop affordability, shield caps, inventory carryover, and health reset.
- AI accuracy across four terrain seeds and a full three-round AI campaign.
- Profile round trips, malformed JSON, invalid types, and failed writes.

Interface coverage
Keyboard and mouse input, slider aiming, firing, AI shot completion,
automatic focus-loss pause, frozen paused physics, resume, round results,
buying ammunition, next round, campaign completion, profile persistence,
hotseat shop handoff and second-player controls, abandon confirmation,
window resizing, letterboxed mouse input, and quit.

Scope and limits
This is a local two-tank game inspired by the legacy title, using original
code/art/audio. The source release requires Python and Pygame; it is not a
signed installer or standalone executable. macOS/Linux and pygame-ce on
Python 3.14+ are supported by the setup instructions but unverified here.
Active campaigns are session-only. Preferences and solo career records save.
Fullscreen has an error fallback; platform-specific fullscreen behavior was
not exercised in these automated checks.
